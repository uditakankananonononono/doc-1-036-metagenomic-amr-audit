## Gates (all required for full-topic metadata GO)

M1 Independent target: measured phenotypic susceptibility (antibiogram/culture or equivalent assay) at an explicitly stated unit (isolate/sample/host/population), documented. Database-derived ARG presence or in-silico genotype calls are features, not labels; labels produced by the same resistance database used for features are circular and FAIL M1/M4. Isolate phenotypes from a sample are NOT the metagenomic community phenotype.
M2 Metagenomic input: community sequencing of a sample (shotgun or equivalent), not isolate genome assemblies. Isolate-only records FAIL M2 as standalone designs.
M3 Sample-level join: documented join between metagenomic input and measured phenotype, with the phenotype's unit (isolate/sample/host/population) and input unit explicitly stated. Metagenome-to-cultured-isolate pairing is a documented design with a coverage limit: cultured taxa are a minority and their phenotype is not the whole community's phenotype. M3 support requires writing that limit; pairing alone is not a pass.
M4 Non-circularity: A label is circular if it is produced, filtered, or curated by any of the databases/tools that supply features, or by a model trained on them; a feature-label pair is circular if both are derived from the same sequence by the same or a derivative database.
Concrete pre-stated cases: (a) ResFinder/RGI phenotype tables and in-silico genotype-to-phenotype calls = circular; (b) EBI AMR merged genotypes-in-silico are features, antibiogram phenotypes are measured, but merge is isolate-level so it fails M2 not M1; (c) susceptibility labels defined by EUCAST/CLSI breakpoints are phenotypic, non-circular; (d) labels from a study that selected samples or called "resistant" by genotype are circular.
M5 External validation support: GO requires >=3 independent cohorts/sites and >=3 species/drug contexts, with independent biological units per cell. Two = UNRESOLVED/scoped. Independent cohort means different site and population, with no shared sample/BioProject/lab protocol unless a common protocol is documented. Species/target contexts require distinct antibiotic classes and distinct taxa. GO requires a documented positive/negative label denominator per drug, otherwise UNKNOWN. Two BioProjects from one group are not independent cohorts unless the record says so; this applies to E. Exploratory single-cohort designs are UNRESOLVED, not GO.
M6 Confounding/terms: documentation permits controlling sequencing platform, host-DNA fraction/depth, clinical setting and database version; reuse terms for sequences and phenotype labels established. UNKNOWN terms stay UNKNOWN; article/tool license does not license sequence or phenotype data. CARD commercial-use restriction and RGI "Other" licence are tool terms only and do not grant redistribution rights, even non-commercial: keep data rights UNKNOWN. No download of CARD/ResFinder database content in F0.

A/B/C cannot satisfy M1-M3 by nature: they are tools/databases, not datasets. Their M1-M3 gates are UNKNOWN-by-construction, not FAIL. Circular pseudo-label use would FAIL M1/M4 if proposed as a design, but no such design is run. D standalone STOP is expected from M2 per its own description. F0's feasibility verdict rests on E and F.
FAIL versus UNKNOWN: affirmative incompatibility requires an explicit statement in the record, e.g. "phenotypes predicted in silico from ResFinder", "isolate genomes only", or "no antibiotic susceptibility testing was performed". Absence of a statement is UNKNOWN, not FAIL.

Admissible designs = one fixed source, or a join the records themselves document (shared identifiers); no inferred overlap or count unions. GO only if all M1-M6 have documentary support for an admissible design. Affirmative incompatibility on a required gate = STOP for that design; missing documentation = UNRESOLVED. Full fixed-set STOP only if every admissible design affirmatively incompatible; otherwise UNRESOLVED. Narrower leads (e.g. isolate genomic AMR prediction, database benchmarking) labelled partial, never promoted, and need a separately registered question. No scientific success regardless of verdict.
